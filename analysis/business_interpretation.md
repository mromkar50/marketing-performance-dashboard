# Business Interpretation — Marketing Performance (Jan–Jun 2025)

**Scope:** 2,700 campaign-day records · 5 channels · 15 campaigns · $1,136,133 ad spend · $3,410,846 attributed revenue · 17,944 new customers.
**Headline:** Blended **ROAS 3.00x** at a **$63.32 CAC**. Efficiency is highly concentrated: two channels produce most of the return, while two consume more than 40% of the budget at weak last-click economics.

---

## 1. Where the money went — and what it earned

| Channel | Spend | % of Budget | Revenue | ROAS | New Cust. | CAC | Proj. 12-mo LTV / customer |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Email | $82,380 | 7.3% | $444,748 | **5.40x** | 2,674 | **$30.81** | $290.22 |
| Paid Search | $248,988 | 21.9% | $1,168,843 | **4.69x** | 4,394 | $56.67 | $320.53 |
| Paid Social | $294,133 | 25.9% | $913,043 | 3.10x | **5,051** | $58.23 | $261.69 |
| Video | $335,718 | **29.5%** | $607,144 | 1.81x | 3,811 | $88.09 | $240.75 |
| Display | $174,915 | 15.4% | $277,068 | 1.58x | 2,014 | $86.85 | $211.91 |

Three observations stand out. First, **Email is the efficiency engine but is severely under-funded**: it produced the highest ROAS (5.40x) and the lowest CAC ($30.81) on just 7.3% of total spend. Second, **Video is the single largest budget line (29.5%) yet returns 1.81x**, and Display is weaker still at 1.58x — together they absorbed 44.9% of spend while delivering only 26% of revenue. Third, **Paid Social is the volume engine**: it acquired the most new customers (5,051, 28% of all acquisitions) at a defensible 3.10x, making it the best scalable growth lever even though it is not the most efficient.

## 2. Trend: quality is improving, Display is not

Overall ROAS improved from **2.92x in Q1 to 3.08x in Q2**. The improvement is driven by Paid Search, which climbed from 4.04x (Jan) to 5.06x (May) — consistent with cumulative optimization of bids, keywords and landing pages. Email held a stable 5.1–5.6x band all six months, confirming that its high return is structural, not a one-off spike. Display, by contrast, drifted *down* from 1.62x to 1.54x with no month above 1.70x, and Video never left the 1.7–1.9x range. Neither awareness channel shows any organic improvement trend, which strengthens the reallocation case.

## 3. Campaign-level: the spread within channels is as big as the spread between them

Ranking all 15 campaigns by ROAS:

- **Top of the table:** Search – Brand Terms (6.04x ROAS, $44.24 CAC) and both core Email programs — Lifecycle Nurture (5.89x) and Newsletter Promo (5.57x). Social – Retargeting Core (4.13x) also earns its budget.
- **Bottom of the table:** Display – Programmatic Reach (1.32x, $104.80 CAC), Display – Publisher Placements (1.27x, $108.12 CAC) and Video – Connected TV Promo (1.49x, $104.41 CAC). These three campaigns spent $225,076 — nearly 20% of the total budget — for a combined 1.38x return.
- **Inside every channel** there is a "core vs. prospecting" gap: retargeting-style programs (Search Brand, Social Retargeting, Display Retargeting 2.31x) outperform their prospecting siblings, which is exactly the pattern expected when retargeting harvests demand that other campaigns created.

## 4. The LTV lens changes the verdict on Video/Display

Last-click ROAS penalizes upper-funnel channels. Measured on projected 12-month LTV instead, every channel recovers its spend: projected LTV-to-spend is 9.42x for Email, 5.66x for Search, 4.49x for Social, 2.73x for Video and 2.44x for Display. In other words, Video and Display are not losing money — every dollar returns $2.44–$2.73 of projected 12-month value — but they are the *most expensive places to keep a marginal dollar* when Email and Search demonstrably turn $1 into $5.66–$9.42. The problem is opportunity cost, not loss.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Reallocate ~15–20% of Display + Video budget toward Email and Search.** Email is capacity-constrained (list size, send frequency), so fund list-growth and lifecycle expansion first; Search has proven it converts incremental spend at ≥4.7x and is still trending up.
2. **Sunset or rework the three bottom campaigns** (Display Programmatic Reach, Display Publisher Placements, Video Connected TV Promo). Redirecting their combined ~$225K at the blended 3.00x rate would model out to roughly +$180K incremental revenue per half-year.
3. **Protect Paid Social's prospecting budget.** It is the only channel acquiring ~5K new customers per half; cutting it to lift blended ROAS would starve future retargeting and email pools.
4. **Judge awareness spend on LTV-to-spend and incrementality, not last-click ROAS** — but require Video/Display to show a rising LTV-to-spend trend (currently flat-to-down) to keep their allocations.
5. **Watch Search Competitor Terms ($79.71 CAC, 3.28x)** — acceptable but the weakest Search program; cap its budget until its CVR closes the gap with Generic Keywords.

## 6. Caveats

- All figures are **synthetic** and generated for portfolio demonstration; they are not real client or employer data.
- Attribution is last-click by construction: Email and Search structurally harvest demand that Social/Video/Display create, so their ROAS advantage is partly an artifact of measurement, not pure operational excellence.
- `projected_12m_ltv_usd` is a forward-looking model estimate per the data dictionary; it is **not** a cohort-measured LTV:CAC ratio (that analysis belongs to Project 3). Ratios are always computed as SUM(numerator) ÷ SUM(denominator), never as averages of daily ratios.
